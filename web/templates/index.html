StreamClip.

Paste a YouTube link — get vertical captioned Shorts.

Free, no sign-up. Clips are made on this machine — a VOD takes a few minutes; live mode is slower here (see limits below).

⚠️ Honest limits

  • Live capture lags on this machine — a 60s live segment can take 2–4 minutes to capture here, so clips trail the live moment. Real-time watching needs a VPS (Oracle free tier or ~$5/mo).
  • This page is local only — no public link yet. A public link needs the app on a VPS with a domain.
  • Auto-upload to TikTok / Shorts / Reels is not built yet — download the clips and post them by hand for now.
  • Captions are one word at a time, centre-cropped 9:16 — side content can be cut off.
