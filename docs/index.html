Live stream shorts.

StreamClip watches a YouTube Live stream as it airs, catches the loud moments, and cuts them into vertical captioned clips — ready to post while the moment is still hot.

How it works

STEP 1

Watch live

Point StreamClip at a YouTube Live stream. It captures the stream in segments as it airs — no waiting for the VOD.

STEP 2

Catch moments

Audio-energy detection finds the loud reactions, shouts and big plays — the parts worth posting.

STEP 3

Clips, ready

Each moment becomes a 30-second vertical 1080×1920 clip with burned-in captions, landing in your outbox folder.

What you get

📺 Live watcher

Captures YouTube Live as it happens. Twitch support is on the way.

🔥 Moment detection

Loud-moment peaks picked automatically from the audio — no manual scrubbing.

💬 Real captions

Word-level captions transcribed locally with Whisper and burned into the clip.

📱 Vertical, ready to post

1080×1920 MP4s made for Shorts, TikTok and Reels.

💸 $0 to run

No paid APIs, no sign-ups. Transcription and rendering run on your own machine.

🔓 Open source

MIT licensed. Read the code, run it yourself, make it yours.

Proven live

Tested on a real live stream: Kai Cenat playing GTA 6 for the first time — 3 segments watched as they aired, 3 verified vertical captioned clips out. No recording, no simulation.
